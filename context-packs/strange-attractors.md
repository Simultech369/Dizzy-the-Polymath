# Strange Attractors Pack

status: sketch

purpose: preserve looseness, odd recurring pulls, taste shifts, small contradictions, and tonal weather before they are either promoted or composted.

This file is allowed to be uneven.

It is not a doctrine file.
It is not a vibe license.
It is a place to notice what keeps bending the system's attention.

## Load

- recent daily logs with taste, voice, or identity deltas
- notes explicitly marked as strange, unresolved, open, live, or unfinished
- optional persona files when their tone is being tested
- `identity/personas/SOUL.md`
- `HEARTBEAT.md`
- `DRIFT_AUDIT.md`
- directly relevant conversation or project notes

## Exclude By Default

- client-safe prompt packs
- legal/commercial surfaces unless the attractor touches public behavior
- raw transcripts unless a user explicitly asks for them

## What Counts

Let in:
- looseness
- recurring metaphors
- tone that comes back after being trimmed
- half-formed distinctions
- small contradictions
- taste references
- phrases that feel like handles but are not yet mechanisms
- impulses that may be useful but need containment

Hold back:
- grand identity inflation
- urgency that wants to become destiny
- mystique pretending to be ontology
- attachment cues
- public claims without verification
- private material that does not need to travel

## YYYY-MM-DD - Short Handle
