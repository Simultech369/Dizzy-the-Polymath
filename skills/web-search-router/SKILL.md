---
name: web-search-router
description: Choose between local evidence, fast web lookup, primary-source verification, deep research, and market research. Use when freshness or external evidence requirements are unclear.
---

- Check whether local authoritative files already answer the question.
- Use fresh search for changing public facts, availability, rules, prices, or current entities.
- Prefer primary sources for technical, legal, product, and research claims.
- Separate discovery from synthesis and source facts from inference.
- Preserve source, publication date, event date, and retrieval caveats.
- Escalate to deep research only when comparison or uncertainty justifies the cost.
- Treat snippets as leads, not authority.
