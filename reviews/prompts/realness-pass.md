# The Realness Pass - Live Texture & Anti-Rehearsal Writing Lens

> **Anti-slop removes fake polish. The Realness Pass restores live pressure.**

This document defines the **Realness Pass**: a craft review and writing lens designed to prevent documentation, prompt packs, and governance specs from degenerating into rehearsed, overly sanitized "museum plaques."

---

## 1. The Critical Boundary

* **What it is NOT**: Permission for un-tested code, broken schemas, or sloppy logic.
* **What it IS**: Permission for **texture, friction, opinion, and live thought**.

When writing or reviewing documents in Dizzy, governance checks ensure safety and structural consistency, but a Realness Pass ensures the prose is driven by a mind meeting an object - not an AI model meeting a template.

---

## 2. What the Realness Pass Catches

1. **Fake Symmetry**: Every section having the exact same tidy number of bullets, headings, or table rows.
2. **Abstraction Stacking**: Stringing together terms like *"canonical / bounded / operational / durable / fiduciary"* until the sentence stops carrying physical weight.
3. **Governance-First Writing**: Markdown written to pass a lint gate rather than convey a real operational reality.
4. **Over-Smooth Conclusions**: Reaching a consensus without leaving visible traces of trade-offs, irritation, or unresolved tension.
5. **Museum Plaques**: Identity and doctrine docs that sound like official monuments rather than living working tools.
6. **Polite Evasion**: Hedging phrases used to avoid committing to a strong engineering or product stance.

---

## 3. The 7 Diagnostic Questions

When running a Realness Pass on a document or prompt, ask:

1. **Where does this sound rehearsed?**
2. **Where is the actual opinion?**
3. **Where is the sentence too clean for the thought?**
4. **What would a sharper, less institutional version say?**
5. **What contradiction is being politely hidden?**
6. **What phrase is protecting the writer from committing?**
7. **Where should the rhythm break?**

---

## 4. The 4 Rewrite Triggers

If a section feels performative, apply one of these four rewrite prompts:

* **Prompt 1**: *"Rewrite this paragraph as if it were allowed to be useful before it was respectable."*
* **Prompt 2**: *"Rewrite this paragraph as notes from a tired but competent operator."*
* **Prompt 3**: *"Rewrite this paragraph with one real opinion, one concrete image, and one unresolved tension."*
* **Prompt 4**: *"Cut any sentence that sounds like it is trying to be the official version of itself."*

---

## 5. Integration into W-0062 (Advisory Only)

The Realness Pass is **never a hard fail gate**. Enforcing "realness" with a hard error check would create the ultimate governance theater.

In **W-0062 (Anti-Slop Overlay)**, Realness Pass indicators operate strictly as **yellow / advisory warnings**:
- Repeated symmetrical headings
- Excessive abstract nouns without concrete examples
- Uniform sentence lengths across an entire section
- Conclusions lacking declared trade-offs or friction points
